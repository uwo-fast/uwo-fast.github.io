These are the FAST group's own lab signs. They are written for one lab, its equipment, its layout and its local procedures, and are published so others can see and adapt them. They are not general safety guidance and they are not a substitute for training.

If you work somewhere else, follow your own workplace procedures and the requirements of your institution and jurisdiction.
